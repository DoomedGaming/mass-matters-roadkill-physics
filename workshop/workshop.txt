description=[h1]Mass Matters: Roadkill Physics - Release 1.2.0[/h1]
description=[b]Compatibility: preserves the Build 42.20 and 42.21 profiles and adds a separate 42.22.2 profile. Original gameplay testing covers 42.20.0. The 42.22.2 engine audit and automated checks pass.[/b]
description=The new profile accepts 42.22, the runtime string reported by the audited 42.22.2 client, and 42.22.2. It skips physics on other runtime strings. A later engine reusing the 42.22 string cannot be distinguished by this guard and still needs an audit. The preserved 42.21 profile still accepts 42.21 and 42.21.0. This package does not add Build 41 support.
description=
description=[h1]Why this mod exists[/h1]
description=Build 42.20 can give tiny animals and bodies a wildly disproportionate effect on vehicles. A live mouse, rat, or rabbit can heavily slow a normal car, while driving over a corpse can pitch or launch even a heavy vehicle. Mass Matters makes those reactions respect the thing being hit instead of treating vehicle mass as the deciding force.
description=
description=[h1]What it changes[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Live animals:[/b] replaces the oversized vehicle reaction with a mass-aware response. This applies to regular cars as well as heavy vehicles.
description=[*][b]Zombie and animal corpses:[/b] replaces disproportionate wheel lift and torque with a mass-aware response.
description=[*][b]Live zombies:[/b] adds a separate setting for the initial horizontal slowdown when a vehicle hits a standing zombie. Keep vanilla slowdown, reduce it, or remove that horizontal impulse.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Sandbox options[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Live animal vehicle reaction[/b] - 0.0 to 2.0; default 1.0 is the intended mass-aware result.
description=[*][b]Corpse vehicle reaction[/b] - 0.0 to 2.0; default 1.0 is the intended mass-aware result.
description=[*][b]Live zombie forward slowdown[/b] - 1.0 keeps vanilla slowdown, 0.5 keeps half, and 0.0 removes the targeted horizontal impulse.
description=[/list]
description=Animal and corpse reactions are capped so the mod cannot make them stronger than the audited vanilla reaction. Multiplayer administrators can apply tuning changes while the server is running; connected clients receive them without reconnecting.
description=
description=[h1]Important live-zombie limitation[/h1]
description=The zombie setting controls the initial horizontal pedestrian impulse. Once a living zombie is prone beneath the wheels, the audited engines use separate private per-wheel lift and torque state that a release-safe Lua mod cannot read or scale. That prone living-zombie reaction remains vanilla and can still affect handling in a crowd. Corpse wheel reactions use a different accessible path and are adjusted by this mod.
description=
